Ghostlight

A digital ofrenda lit by a local ghost.

For Día de los Muertos, we build an altar so the ones we lost can find their way back for one evening. Ghostlight keeps that altar on your own machine: leave a name, an offering and a memory, and Gemma 3 — an open-weight model running entirely on your computer — writes the words. Nothing is uploaded. Grief is private.
